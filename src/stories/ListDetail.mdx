# List-detail

A list-detail page keeps a collection on the left and one of its records on the
right: users, companies, the applications whose access is being granted. The
list stays in view while the record changes, so moving from one record to the
next is one click.

The application writes the values, as in *Lists*.

## The list

The list pane is 300px wide, on `content-bg`, with a `content-border` line on
its right. From top to bottom, each part a band:

1. **Title row.** The name of the page and, when records can be created, a
   quiet `Button variant="outlined" semantic="neutral"` at `text-sm`: "New".
   The filled `primary` belongs to the detail.
2. **Search,** full width, and under it a `RadioBar` when the collection has
   states (enabled, disabled).
3. **Caption** with the count, as in *Lists*.
4. **Rows.** An identity (an `Avatar` for a person, a rounded square for a
   thing), the name in `font-semibold`, a second line in `text-sm text-label`,
   and at most one short fact at the right in `text-xs text-label`. Each row is
   a link. The selected row takes `bg-primary-100` and keeps `text-content`;
   hover is `bg-neutral-100`. The focus ring shows on `:focus-visible` only, so
   a click leaves the selection, not a ring.

While a record is being created, a provisional row ("New user", "not saved")
sits at the top of the list as the selected one.

## The detail

### Container

Everything in the detail is read inside one container: a column at most 54rem
wide, centred, with `px-8`. Every band of the detail uses it for its content
(the header, the tabs, each tab, the save bar), so their edges fall on the same
two verticals. The lines between bands still run from edge to edge of the
pane, as *Bands* states.

Tables and summary strips are data and take the full width; a form is read in
a column and takes the container.

### Header

The header names the record: its identity at 56px (a circle for a person, a
square with radius 14px for a thing), the name as the title in `text-2xl
font-semibold tracking-tighter`, and under it one line of context in
`text-label` (alias, role, email; tax id, country, currency). The status of the
record sits at the right, as in *Lists*.

### Tabs

A record with more than one screen of content is split into `Tabs` under the
header, one per part of the record: profile, the companies it sees, the
applications it opens, its password. Each tab fits on one screen. A record
being created has no tabs: creating asks only for what is needed to exist, in
the same layout as the profile tab.

### Forms

A tab of fields groups them under short headings (`text-base font-semibold`),
with no description column and no lines between groups. Fields sit two to a row
in the container. Each uses `Control`: the label above, the message and the
error below, as `Control` draws them. A value
that cannot change is plain text, not a disabled field. A set of values, such
as secondary currencies, is a row of removable chips and one select to add.

### Access

Granting access is a pair of lists, the same from either side: from the
person (the companies she sees, the applications she opens) and from the thing
(who sees this company, who opens this application).

- **Has access,** each row with its identity, its name, its role as a
  `RadioBar` when access has degrees (Member, Admin), and "Remove" as text.
  The heading carries, at its right, one line on what the access or the roles
  mean.
- **No access,** each row in `text-label` with "Give access" as
  `Button variant="outlined" semantic="neutral"`. An empty list says so in a
  sentence.

### Saving

A form saves with a sticky bar at the bottom of the detail: "Unsaved changes"
at the left in `text-label`, then "Discard" as text and "Save" as the filled
`primary`. The bar shows on the tabs that have fields. A tab whose only content
is an action, such as changing a password, carries that action as its primary
and no bar.

Access saves at once: each "Give access", "Remove" or role change is applied
when it is made, and a toast confirms it with an undo. A page of access has no
save bar.
